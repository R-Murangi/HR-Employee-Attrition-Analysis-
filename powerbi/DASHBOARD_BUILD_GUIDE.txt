POWER BI DASHBOARD — EXACT VISUAL SETUP

After loading data/HR_PowerBI_Ready.csv, rename the table HR.
Create the measures in measures.dax ONE AT A TIME (Modeling > New measure).
Select Attrition Rate measure > Measure tools > Format > Percentage > 1 decimal.

PAGE TITLE: HR Employee Attrition Dashboard

TOP ROW: Four Card visuals
Card 1: [Total Employees] (1470)
Card 2: [Employees Who Left] (237)
Card 3: [Attrition Rate] (16.1%)
Card 4: [Average Monthly Income] (~6503)

CHART 1: Clustered bar chart
Y-axis: HR[Department]
X-axis: [Employees Who Left]
Title: Employees Who Left by Department
Expected: Research & Development 133, Sales 92, Human Resources 12.

CHART 2: Clustered bar chart
Y-axis: HR[JobRole]
X-axis: [Employees Who Left]
Title: Employees Who Left by Job Role

CHART 3: Donut chart
Legend: HR[OverTime]
Values: [Employees Who Left]
Title: Attrition by Overtime
Expected: Yes 127, No 110, total 237 (when no chart is selected).

CHART 4: Clustered column chart
X-axis: HR[AgeGroup]
Y-axis: [Attrition Rate]
Title: Attrition Rate by Age Group
The AgeGroup column is already in the CSV. No new column formula needed.

CHART 5: Clustered bar chart
Y-axis: HR[JobSatisfaction]
X-axis: [Attrition Rate]
Title: Attrition Rate by Job Satisfaction

CHART 6: Clustered bar chart
Y-axis: HR[IncomeGroup]
X-axis: [Attrition Rate]
Title: Attrition Rate by Income Group

SLICERS: Add separate slicer visuals using HR[Department], HR[Gender], HR[OverTime].

NOTE: Clicking a bar in one chart filters other visuals. Click blank canvas
or deselect the bar to see full-dataset counts again.

FINISH: File > Save As > HR_Analytics_Dashboard.pbix in this powerbi folder.

The actual report visuals must be placed in Power BI Desktop; this ZIP
provides ready-to-load data, calculations, styling and exact chart settings.
